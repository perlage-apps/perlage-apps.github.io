Support

Fragen, Ideen oder ein Fehler gefunden? Schreib uns an info@perlage-innovation.de.

Wie spiele ich mit jemandem zusammen?

Öffne ein Brettspiel (Four in a Row, Reversi, Dame, Mühle oder Schach) und wähle: auf diesem Gerät, gegen den Computer, in der Nähe, online oder SharePlay.

Warum fragt die App nach dem lokalen Netzwerk?

Nur für „In der Nähe“: So finden sich zwei Geräte direkt, ohne Internet.

Was ist die Herausforderung des Tages?

Jeden Tag gibt es ein Spiel, das für alle auf der Welt gleich verteilt ist. Wer in Game Center angemeldet ist, landet mit seiner Zeit in der Rangliste.

Was ist der Joker-Tag?

Verpasst du einen Tag, rettet der Joker deine Serie. Danach ist er nach 7 Tagen wieder bereit.

Wie stelle ich einen Kauf wieder her?

Einstellungen (Zahnrad) › Werbefrei › „Käufe wiederherstellen“.

Wie ändere ich die Sprache?

Einstellungen (Zahnrad) › Sprache. Standard ist die Sprache deines Geräts.

Wird mein Fortschritt gesichert?

Fortschritt und Statistik liegen nur auf deinem Gerät. Wenn du die App löschst, sind sie weg.

Questions, ideas or found a bug? Write to us at info@perlage-innovation.de.

How do I play with someone?

Open a board game (Four in a Row, Reversi, Checkers, Mill or Chess) and choose: on this device, against the computer, nearby, online or SharePlay.

Why does the app ask for local network access?

Only for “Nearby”: this lets two devices find each other directly, without the internet.

What is the challenge of the day?

Every day there is one game dealt the same way for everyone in the world. If you are signed in to Game Center, your time goes on the leaderboard.

What is the joker day?

If you miss a day, the joker saves your streak. It is ready again after 7 days.

How do I restore a purchase?

Settings (gear) › Ad-free › “Restore purchases”.

How do I change the language?

Settings (gear) › Language. By default the app uses your device language.

Is my progress backed up?

Progress and statistics are stored only on your device. If you delete the app, they are gone.

No collection of personal data

Perlage Games does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. There is no user account with us, no third-party advertising, no tracking and no analytics or statistics services. We do not operate our own servers. Notes about other apps from Perlage Apps are shown via Apple's App Store; we do not collect any data in doing so.

Storage on the device

Your game settings (e.g. difficulty, language, card design) as well as game statistics and progress (wins, streaks, best times, Pearl Level, achievements) are stored only locally on your device. This data never leaves the device and is removed when you delete the app.

Permissions

For playing “Nearby”, the app asks for access to the local network. The app only uses notifications if you turn on the reminder for the daily challenge yourself; the reminder is scheduled locally on your device. The app does not need any other permissions, such as camera, microphone, photos, contacts or location.

Nickname and playing together

The app stores your nickname and symbol only on your device. When you play with others, your nickname, symbol and moves are transmitted only to the people you play with. We do not receive this data.

Nearby

When playing “Nearby”, the devices connect directly via Wi-Fi or Bluetooth (Apple Multipeer Connectivity), without internet and without servers.

Game Center and SharePlay

Online games run via Apple Game Center, playing together in a FaceTime call via Apple SharePlay. If you are signed in to Game Center, your time in the daily challenge (leaderboard) and your achievements are sent to Game Center. Apple processes data such as your Game Center name under its own responsibility; Apple's privacy policy applies. We do not receive this data.

Children

The app may also be used by children. Since no personal data is collected, no data of children is processed either.

If you contact us by email, we use your details only to answer your request. The message is not passed on to third parties and is deleted as soon as it is no longer needed.
