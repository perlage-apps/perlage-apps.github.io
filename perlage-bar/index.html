Support

Dir fehlt ein Drink, etwas klappt nicht oder du hast eine Idee? Schreib uns an info.perlage-apps@web.de – oder direkt aus der App unter Einstellungen › „Mit uns kann man reden“.

Wie stelle ich einen Kauf wieder her?

Einstellungen (Zahnrad) › Vollversion › „Käufe wiederherstellen“.

Wie ändere ich die Sprache?

Einstellungen (Zahnrad) › Sprache. Standard ist die Sprache deines Geräts.

Wo sind meine Daten?

Partys, Einkaufslisten, Vorrat und eigene Rezepte liegen auf deinem Gerät – und, wenn du in iCloud angemeldet bist, in deinem privaten iCloud-Speicher.

Missing a drink, something not working, or have an idea? Write to us at info.perlage-apps@web.de – or right from the app under Settings › “We're easy to talk to”.

How do I restore a purchase?

Settings (gear) › Full version › “Restore purchases”.

How do I change the language?

Settings (gear) › Language. By default the app uses your device language.

Where is my data?

Parties, shopping lists, supplies and your own recipes are stored on your device – and, if you are signed in to iCloud, in your private iCloud storage.

Privacy in brief

Perlage Bar does not collect any data. Your party selection, number of guests, shopping list, your supplies and the menu code stay only on this device. There is no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools. If you dictate using the keyboard microphone, Apple's dictation feature handles this; the app itself records nothing.

What data is stored on the device

  • Your parties: name, date, selected drinks and their share, number of guests, drinks per guest, notes
  • Ticked items on the shopping list
  • Your supplies (ingredients you enter) and your favourites
  • Your own and edited recipes, including photos you add

The app only uses the camera and photos when you trigger it (your own drink photo, supplies scan). Recognition of labels and ingredients runs entirely on the device (Apple Vision or Apple Intelligence on the device); photos are not uploaded. A PDF shopping list you create is only shared if you do so yourself.

Voice input: when you tap “Speak”, the app uses the microphone and Apple's speech recognition – directly on the device if your device supports it; otherwise Apple processes the recording according to its privacy policy. The app does not store any recordings.

iCloud: if you are signed in to iCloud, the app syncs parties, favourites, supplies and your own recipes (without photos) between your devices via your private iCloud storage. We as the provider have no access to it.

This data is kept in the app's protected storage area. We as the provider have no access to it; it is not transmitted to us or to third parties. The legal basis is providing the functions you request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). The menu code is not stored permanently.

Apple

The app is distributed through Apple's App Store. When you download it, Apple processes data under its own responsibility (Apple's privacy policy). We may only receive aggregated, anonymous download and crash statistics from Apple if you have agreed to sharing them in the iOS settings.

Storage period and deletion

The data remains stored until you delete it – in the app with “Delete all data” or by deleting the app.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR). As we do not receive any data from you, you can exercise these rights directly in the app. Questions to info.perlage-apps@web.de. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, e.g.: Die Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz Niedersachsen, Prinzenstraße 5, 30159 Hannover, Germany.

This website

The drinks menu that guests open via QR code (karte.html) is displayed entirely in the browser. Which drinks are shown is contained only in the link itself; it is not transmitted to us. There are no cookies, no analytics and no content from third-party servers.
